# AI Company & UAT Profiling Agent

Tudor built an AI-oriented company and UAT profiling project in a photovoltaic business context.

UAT refers to Romanian administrative-territorial units.

The project was designed to gather and organize information relevant to evaluating companies and administrative areas.

Capabilities include:

- company profiling
- UAT profiling
- retrieval of financial or business information
- evaluation of photovoltaic opportunity or suitability
- structured reporting
- PDF report generation
- support for commercial outreach
- AI-generated marketing or outreach content

A solar suitability scoring approach ranging from 0 to 100 was incorporated into the project.

Technologies associated with the project include:

- Python
- Streamlit
- APIs
- data processing
- AI / LLM functionality

The purpose of the project is to combine data gathering, analysis, AI, and automation to support business workflows.
